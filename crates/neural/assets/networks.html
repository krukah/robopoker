The logits are example values to drag, not trained weights; the readout after them is exact. Before advantages[p] is first fit, est_regret is 0 at every choice and matched plays uniform. Before strategies is fit, est_strategy is uniform. The width and depth sliders change only this page; the nets read DeepHyperParams::get().

Which net answers, epoch by epoch

crates/neural/src/solver.rs · DeepSolver::step

Each epoch runs K traversals against the nets as they stand. Regret rows from the walker's infosets go to its M_V,p; strategy rows, σᵗ at the opponents' infosets, go to M_Π. Then only the walker's advantages[p] refits, from fresh weights, on its whole memory. strategies fits once, after the last epoch. Every row carries t = epoch / players + 1 as its loss weight.
